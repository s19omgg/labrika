# LABRICA Landing Design Specification

## Product

LABRICA is a B2B content operating system.

It combines:
- content creation
- LABRICA AI
- Brand Brain
- images and video
- approvals
- content calendar
- publishing
- analytics
- integrations

## Design direction

The landing should feel:

- precise
- editorial
- technological
- calm
- premium
- product-led
- minimal

Avoid:

- generic SaaS layouts
- excessive rounded cards
- bento grids without purpose
- glassmorphism
- purple AI gradients
- fake dashboards
- fake testimonials
- fake logos
- excessive pills
- decorative AI imagery

## Brand

Preserve:
- LABRICA identity
- light-first visual language

## Core principle

Show the product instead of describing the product.

Use real LABRICA interface elements wherever possible.

## Page structure

1. Header
2. Hero
3. Product proof
4. Workflow
5. LABRICA AI
6. Brand Brain
7. Team and approvals
8. Planning and publishing
9. Analytics
10. Integrations
11. Pricing
12. FAQ
13. Final CTA
14. Footer

## Product story

Idea
→ generation
→ Brand Brain
→ review
→ approval
→ planning
→ publishing
→ analytics
→ learning

## Motion

Use motion selectively.

Preferred:
- line text reveal
- word reveal
- subtle product UI transitions
- section entrances
- spring-like hover interactions
- one major scroll-driven product story

Avoid:
- constant parallax
- excessive floating elements
- animations that do not communicate product behavior

Use prefers-reduced-motion.

## Responsive

Desktop:
product storytelling may use sticky layouts.

Mobile:
all sticky storytelling must gracefully convert into vertical chapters.

Never sacrifice readability for visual density.
